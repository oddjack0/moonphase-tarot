Aquarius

January 20 – February 18
The water-bearer pouring stars for everyone
Element · Air Ruled by · Uranus Modality · Fixed Traits · original, humane, contrarian

The Aquarius Spirit

Aquarius is the zodiac's visionary — a fixed air sign ruled by Uranus, planet of revolution and awakening. The Water-Bearer pours out knowledge for the collective, and Aquarius thinks in exactly those terms: systems, futures, humanity. Original to the bone, this sign would rather be interesting than approved of, and usually manages both.

Friendly, principled and gloriously eccentric, Aquarius natives are loyal to their people and their ideals in equal measure. Their lesson is intimacy: letting one person at a time see past the ideas to the heart that holds them.

Aquarius in Your Big Three

Sun in Aquarius

The Core

You are wired for the future. Aquarius Suns think in systems, question every rule and champion the collective.

Moon in Aquarius

The Heart

Your emotions run through your ideals. You need intellectual kinship and space to be exactly yourself.

Aquarius Rising

The Aura

You seem intriguing, unconventional and friendly-but-independent — people can't quite categorize you, and that's the point.

Light

Strengths

  • Originality — genuinely new thinking.
  • Humanitarianism — the group chat is humanity.
  • Independence — unowned by trends or pressure.
Shadow

Challenges

  • Detachment — feelings processed as concepts.
  • Contrarianism — rebellion as reflex.
  • Fixed ideas — the revolutionary can resist updating.
Love

Aquarius in Love

In love, Aquarius falls for the mind and stays for the friendship. They need a partner who is also their favorite conversationalist, who honors their freedom and shares their causes. Conventional romance bores them; a meeting of true minds is their great love story.

Work

Aquarius at Work

Aquarius thrives in technology, science, social change, media and community-building — work aimed at the future. They are the ones sketching the world everyone else will live in.
