Taurus

April 20 – May 20
The garden that outlasts every storm
Element · Earth Ruled by · Venus Modality · Fixed Traits · steady, sensual, loyal

The Taurus Spirit

Taurus is the zodiac's master builder — a fixed earth sign ruled by Venus, planet of beauty and value. Where Aries sparks, Taurus sustains: slow, deliberate and nearly immovable once committed. This is the sign of the senses — good food, soft light, music with weight — and of wealth in every form that can be held, grown and kept.

Taurus natives are loyal past the point of reason and patient past the point of most people's endurance. Their lesson is flexibility: knowing when holding on is devotion and when it has quietly become fear of change.

Taurus in Your Big Three

Sun in Taurus

The Core

You build slowly and keep what you build. Taurus Suns value comfort, quality and results you can touch.

Moon in Taurus

The Heart

Your heart settles through the senses — good food, familiar places, unhurried affection. Security is your love language.

Taurus Rising

The Aura

You appear calm, grounded and quietly luxurious. People trust your steadiness before they know anything else about you.

Light

Strengths

  • Endurance — Taurus finishes, beautifully and completely.
  • Loyalty — their people are their people, for good.
  • Craft — an instinct for quality in everything they touch.
Shadow

Challenges

  • Stubbornness — the same root that grounds them can trap them.
  • Possessiveness — love and fear of loss can blur.
  • Comfort zones — growth usually waits just outside them.
Love

Taurus in Love

In love, Taurus is sensual, devoted and all-in. Courtship is slow and delicious; commitment, once given, is bedrock. They show love through presence, touch and providing — and they need to feel chosen, every day.

Work

Taurus at Work

Taurus excels in finance, craft, food, land, beauty and any field where patience compounds. They are natural accumulators — of skill, savings and reputation — and their work ages well.
