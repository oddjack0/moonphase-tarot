Sagittarius

November 22 – December 21
The archer whose arrow is aimed at truth
Element · Fire Ruled by · Jupiter Modality · Mutable Traits · honest, restless, philosophical

The Sagittarius Spirit

Sagittarius is the zodiac's explorer — a mutable fire sign ruled by Jupiter, planet of expansion and luck. The Archer aims at horizons: geographic, intellectual, spiritual. Half horse, half human, Sagittarius is instinct reaching for meaning — the traveler who reads philosophy, the comedian who tells the truth sideways.

Optimistic, blunt and allergic to cages, Sagittarius natives carry the fire of conviction. Their lesson is aim: learning that freedom and commitment aren't opposites, and that the honest word lands better with a little aim and timing.

Sagittarius in Your Big Three

Sun in Sagittarius

The Core

You are the seeker. Sagittarius Suns chase horizons — travel, truth and big ideas feed your fire.

Moon in Sagittarius

The Heart

Your heart needs freedom and meaning. New places, big questions and laughter are your emotional medicine.

Sagittarius Rising

The Aura

You seem open, adventurous and refreshingly blunt. People expect stories — and honesty — from you.

Light

Strengths

  • Optimism — faith in life, renewed daily.
  • Honesty — the truth, unvarnished and kind underneath.
  • Vision — they see the big picture instinctively.
Shadow

Challenges

  • Restlessness — arrival can feel like a trap.
  • Bluntness — honesty without a filter bruises.
  • Overpromising — enthusiasm writes checks focus must cash.
Love

Sagittarius in Love

In love, Sagittarius is warm, funny and freedom-loving — a partner in adventure more than a keeper of routines. They fall for laughter and shared horizons, and they stay where the conversation keeps growing. Clinginess ends things; curiosity renews them.

Work

Sagittarius at Work

Sagittarius thrives in travel, publishing, teaching, law, sport and entrepreneurship — work with horizons in it. They are the zodiac's evangelists: whatever they believe in, they sell with their whole chest.
