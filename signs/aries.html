Aries

March 21 – April 19
The first spark of the zodiac
Element · Fire Ruled by · Mars Modality · Cardinal Traits · bold, driven, direct

The Aries Spirit

Aries is where the astrological year ignites. Ruled by Mars, the planet of drive and desire, this cardinal fire sign doesn't wait for permission or perfect conditions — it moves, and the path appears under its feet. Aries energy is raw beginnings: the first green shoot, the opening move, the honest word said before anyone else dares.

At their best, Aries natives are courage incarnate — loyal, straightforward and allergic to pretense. Their work in this life is learning that the strongest fire is the one that knows where to burn: passion with aim, courage with patience, and a temper transformed into fuel.

Aries in Your Big Three

Sun in Aries

The Core

You lead with courage and instinct. Aries Suns are self-starters who would rather act and adjust than wait for permission.

Moon in Aries

The Heart

Your feelings arrive fast and burn bright. You need movement, honesty and a little healthy competition to feel emotionally alive.

Aries Rising

The Aura

You come across as energetic, frank and unafraid — people meet the warrior first, then discover the warmth underneath.

Light

Strengths

  • Fearless initiative — Aries starts what others only discuss.
  • Honesty — what you see is exactly what you get.
  • Resilience — knocked down seven times, standing by eight.
Shadow

Challenges

  • Impatience — wanting it now can burn bridges built for later.
  • Follow-through — beginnings thrill them; middles must be learned.
  • Temper — quick to flare, though equally quick to forgive.
Love

Aries in Love

In love, Aries is direct, passionate and playfully competitive. They fall fast and love out loud, and they need a partner who respects their independence while matching their fire. Boredom is the enemy; shared adventure is the glue.

Work

Aries at Work

Aries thrives where initiative is rewarded: founding, leading, competing, creating. They are the zodiac's pioneers — first through the door, happiest when the mission is bold and the leash is short.
