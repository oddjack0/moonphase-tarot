Leo

July 23 – August 22
The Sun's sign — born to shine, built to warm
Element · Fire Ruled by · Sun Modality · Fixed Traits · radiant, proud, generous

The Leo Spirit

Leo is the only sign ruled by the Sun, and like its ruler it is a fixed point of light: warm, radiant and impossible to ignore. This fixed fire sign doesn't chase the spotlight so much as generate it — Leo is creative self-expression as a life force, joy performed and generosity staged on a grand scale.

Beneath the roar is one of the zodiac's biggest hearts. Leo protects its pride — children, lovers, creations, friends — with theatrical loyalty. Their lesson is that true radiance needs no audience: the Sun shines on the empty desert too.

Leo in Your Big Three

Sun in Leo

The Core

You were born to shine. Leo Suns create, perform and protect with a huge heart and an unapologetic need to be seen.

Moon in Leo

The Heart

Your emotions need an audience of loved ones — appreciation and play are emotional oxygen for you.

Leo Rising

The Aura

You appear confident, warm and magnetic. People notice your presence before you say a word.

Light

Strengths

  • Generosity — grand, warm and genuinely meant.
  • Creative fire — everything they touch gets more alive.
  • Loyalty — their pride is protected like family.
Shadow

Challenges

  • Pride — criticism can land like insult.
  • Drama — feelings arrive at theatrical scale.
  • Recognition hunger — applause can become a need.
Love

Leo in Love

In love, Leo is romantic, playful and devoted — a grand lover who courts with flair and adores being adored in return. They need appreciation the way plants need sun, and they give back tenfold in warmth, loyalty and fun.

Work

Leo at Work

Leo belongs on stages of every kind: performance, leadership, design, branding, anything with an audience and a standard of excellence. They make work feel like an event.
